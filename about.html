  • what is an apsara???

    Apsara is a class of celestial, feminine beings in Hindu and Buddhist cultures, originally water and cloud spirits, also known as celestial nymphs, angels, or fairies. There are thousands of Apsaras carved in Cambodia's Angkor Wat, which inspired the classical Khmer dance Robam Tep Apsara. I was named after this dance. But please don't ask me to perform it!
