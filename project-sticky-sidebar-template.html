The Crow of Tonle Sap

Inspired by Aesop’s fable, The Crow and the Pitcher, this is a linocut print made during a 3rd year workshop at IDEA.

Because my mother used to read me this fable from a Khmer language textbook, I gave this piece some Cambodian flair. The stilt house is a reference to the floating villages of Siem Reap, and the pebbles say “caw!” in Khmer script. I guess I kinda turned the crow itself into the “pitcher”, containing an entire lake…

I was also very inspired by @morn_chear_art and I like how he does houses in his block prints, so I borrowed that carving technique for the roof of my house. អរគុណ! And many thanks to @khenry_art for your guidance and advice!
